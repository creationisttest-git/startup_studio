# Licence

**AGPL-3.0.** The full text is in `LICENSE`.

In plain terms, and this is a summary rather than the licence itself:

You can use this, change it, and build on it, including commercially. If you distribute a
modified version, or run one as a service that other people use over a network, you have
to make your changes available under the same licence.

That last part is the whole reason for this choice. The point of the studio is that an
improvement made anywhere reaches everyone, and a permissive licence would have let a
modified version go closed while the page still asked people to share their improvements
back. The licence and the request now say the same thing.

If AGPL does not suit what you want to do, get in touch at projectfreedom.xyz. A different
arrangement is usually possible.
